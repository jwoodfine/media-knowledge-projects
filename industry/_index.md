## Woodfine's Structural Policies

[[regional-market-capital-gap|The Regional Market Capital Gap]] documents a structural shortfall in construction and take-out financing in Regional Markets, despite Metro-comparable incomes — the gap Woodfine's Direct-Hold Solutions are designed to address. [[supply-discipline|Supply Discipline and Full Occupancy]] is Woodfine's deliberate policy of sizing each building to identified Regional Market demand rather than to maximum permissible area, producing full occupancy, documented waiting lists, and preserved pricing power.
