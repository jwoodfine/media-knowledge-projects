Woodfine's direct-hold portfolio is intended to expand across New Europe — Czechia, Hungary, Slovakia, Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, and Greece — as part of the broader [[rollout-index|rollout program]]. This article describes the planned New Europe programme; it is not a record of current sites, committed timing, or a finalized regional map.

## Planned scope

The New Europe programme is intended to define [[development-regions|development regions]] within each of the seven countries — the geographic and jurisdictional zones within which co-location candidates are evaluated and compliance postures, including applicable securities regulation in each country, are applied. As those regions are formally defined, this article will be updated to reflect them.

## Site tracking as the programme matures

Candidate sites identified across New Europe will be tracked through the active development pipeline and, where adjacent land is not yet available, the [[about-development-site-monitoring-register|development site monitoring register]], as the [[co-location-methodology|co-location methodology]] identifies qualifying locations. Required development counts for each New Europe direct-hold vehicle are documented in the monitoring register; this article does not restate or expand on that site-level detail, which is subject to change as the programme develops.

## Status

The New Europe programme's status is planned. This article asserts no site counts, regions, or dates beyond what is already established in the monitoring register and development-regions articles, and will be expanded as the programme's regional structure is formally set.
