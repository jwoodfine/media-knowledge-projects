Eagan, a Dakota County suburb on the south bank of the Minnesota River, has one Regional-tier
co-location cluster. It is not among the markets published for North America under the
confirmed 2026-08-07 dataset. A corporate office, distribution, and logistics base —
including the Minnesota Vikings' headquarters and training facility — distinguishes it from a
purely residential suburb.

## Overview

Eagan recorded a population of 68,855 at the 2020 census (a 2022 estimate of 67,534) across
33.46 square miles. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> It sits south of Saint
Paul (a general Wikipedia geographic description) on the Minnesota River upstream from its
confluence with the Mississippi. Originally an Irish farming community once known as the
Onion Capital of the United States, Eagan has transformed into a mixed commercial hub of
corporate offices, distribution centres, and healthcare operations. The Minnesota Vikings
relocated their headquarters and training facility to Eagan in 2018; the associated Twin
Cities Orthopedics Performance Center spans 277,000 square feet with practice fields and a
6,500-seat outdoor stadium.
