Modena is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with three Tier 1
co-location clusters — the strongest anchor composition of any market in Italy's published set.

## Overview

Modena is a city and comune on the south side of the Po Valley, in the Province of Modena, in
the region of Emilia-Romagna in northern Italy, with a population of 184,361 as of 2026 — the
20th-largest city in Italy. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
