Zadar is the oldest continuously inhabited city in Croatia, on the Adriatic coast in Dalmatia. It
is anchored by Kaufland and Interspar (hypermarket), Bauhaus and Pevex (hardware), and Decathlon
(sport) — one Regional-tier co-location cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier.
Ancona, Italy, sits 179.1 kilometres away by straight-line distance, crossing the Adriatic Sea
rather than reflecting a land commuting relationship. The market carries European Regional Market
classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Zadar recorded 70,779 residents at the 2021 census. <!-- wikipedia-sourced:
population/description --> The city traces its origins to the 9th century BC as a Liburnian
settlement, became a Roman colony in 48 BC, and later served as capital of the Byzantine Theme of
Dalmatia, establishing itself as a major Mediterranean trading centre. Zadar was designated a
UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2017 as part of the Venetian defensive fortifications system built
across the 16th and 17th centuries.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 2.7 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and sport anchor. Kaufland
and Interspar, hypermarket chains comparable to Walmart or Kroger, each hold two locations within
the cluster, alongside Bauhaus and Pevex (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and
Decathlon (sport).

Civic anchors include Zadar General Hospital and the University of Zadar, whose presence in the
cluster extends to a dedicated new campus.
