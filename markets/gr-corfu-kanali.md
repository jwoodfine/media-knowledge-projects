Kanali is a community on the island of Corfu in the Ionian Sea. It is anchored by Sklavenitis
(hypermarket), Kotsovolos (electronics), and Praktiker (hardware) — one Regional-tier co-location
cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Patras sits 219.8 kilometres away by
straight-line distance — the longest metropolitan reference among Greece's seven current entries,
crossing open water from Corfu to the Greek mainland. Kanali functions within Corfu's own local
economy rather than as a functional suburb of Patras; the reference distance reflects the
framework's nearest-continental-reference-point rule, not a commuting relationship.

## Overview

Kanali recorded 4,768 residents at the 2021 census, within the municipality of Central Corfu and
the Diapontian Islands. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> Kanali functions as
part of Corfu city's urban area and has grown steadily from roughly 2,700 residents in 1991,
reflecting gradual island development rather than rapid metropolitan expansion.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 4.8 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and electronics anchor.
Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds two locations within
the cluster, paired with one Kotsovolos electronics store and one Praktiker hardware store.

Civic weight comes from Ionian University, which maintains four separate buildings in the area —
its central campus, rectorate, and two historic sites, a former psychiatric hospital and the old
fortress, repurposed for university use.
