Boca del Río is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with two Tier 1
co-location clusters.

## Overview

Boca del Río is a city and municipality located in the centre of the Mexican state of
Veracruz, lying just south of the municipality of Veracruz. It is a port in its own right and
serves as the metropolitan area's centre for business travel.
