Bodø is a city on a peninsula in Norway's far north, the administrative centre of Nordland
county. It is anchored by OBS Coop (hypermarket), XXL (sport), and Elkjøp and Power (electronics)
across a single Regional-tier co-location cluster. Trondheim sits 466.6 kilometres away by
straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set — the largest
reference distance among Norway's nine current entries, a consequence of Bodø's position north of
the Arctic Circle rather than any gap in the underlying data. The market carries European Regional
Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Bodø recorded 43,504 residents in 2025, on a peninsula between two fjords just north of the
Arctic Circle. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The town serves as
administrative centre for both its municipality and Nordland county, and experiences the midnight
sun from June through mid-July. Nearby Saltstraumen carries some of the strongest tidal currents
in the world, with speeds reaching 22 knots. Bodø was designated a European Capital of Culture for
2024 and is home to Bodø/Glimt, one of Norway's most successful football clubs.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 0.3 kilometres and combines OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable
to Walmart or Kroger) with XXL (sport) and two electronics chains, Elkjøp and Power (comparable to
Best Buy).

Civic weight comes from two hospitals within the cluster: Nordlandssykehuset, the regional
hospital serving Nordland county, and Rønvik sykehus. No university anchor is recorded here.

## AEC Data

Bodø's cluster sits in a Dfc (subarctic) Köppen climate zone, with an annual mean temperature of
4.2°C. The site records 5,069 heating degree days and 13 cooling degree days a year (base 18°C).
Wind speed and solar irradiance (GHI) are not recorded for this cluster in the current dataset.

Seismic exposure is low, with peak ground acceleration reaching 0.0164g, and wildfire hazard is
classified low. The surrounding ecoregion is the Scandinavian coastal conifer forests, part of the
Temperate Conifer Forests biome.
