Drammen is a port city in Buskerud county, 39.7 kilometres southwest of Oslo. It combines a
Regional-tier cluster — OBS Coop paired with three hardware chains and Elkjøp — and two
Local-tier clusters built around Montér, XL-Bygg, Elkjøp, and Würth. Oslo sits 39.7 kilometres
away by straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set. The market
carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Drammen recorded 106,013 residents as of the fourth quarter of 2025, on the Drammenselva river in
southeastern Norway. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city formed in 1811
from three separate seaports and grew into a major maritime and timber-trading hub; a 2020 merger
with Svelvik and Nedre Eiker municipalities made it Norway's seventh-largest city, with more than
100,000 residents. Drammen has won 21 environmental and urban-development awards since 2003, more
than any other Norwegian city.

## Anchor Composition

The Regional-tier cluster spans 1.9 kilometres and pairs OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable to
Walmart or Kroger) with three hardware chains — OBS Bygg, Byggtorget, and XL-Bygg (comparable to
Home Depot or Lowe's) — and Elkjøp (electronics, comparable to Best Buy).

Two Local-tier clusters sit roughly eight and twelve kilometres from the Regional-tier cluster,
and under four kilometres from each other. Both combine hardware and electronics anchors — Montér
and XL-Bygg respectively, alongside Elkjøp — with Würth (industrial and MRO supply, comparable to
Grainger). Both also share the same civic anchors: Drammen sykehus, the city's hospital, and the
University of South-Eastern Norway's Drammen campus.
