Ullensaker is a market in Norway's Romerike region, in Akershus county. It is anchored by OBS
Coop (hypermarket), two hardware chains — OBS Bygg and Montér — XXL (sport), and Würth (industrial
and MRO supply) across a single Regional-tier co-location cluster. Oslo sits 34.6 kilometres away
by straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set, the closest of
Norway's nine current reference distances. The market carries European Regional Market
classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Ullensaker recorded 43,814 residents in 2023, in the Romerike region north of Oslo. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The municipality hosts Oslo Airport, Gardermoen,
Norway's largest international airport, which has driven substantial population growth around its
administrative centre, Jessheim. Ullensaker is also home to Raknehaugen, the largest burial mound
in the Nordic countries, radiocarbon-dated to roughly 550 CE.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 1.7 kilometres and combines OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable
to Walmart or Kroger) with two hardware chains, OBS Bygg and Montér (comparable to Home Depot or
Lowe's), XXL (sport), and Würth (industrial and MRO supply, comparable to Grainger).

Civic weight comes from two medical facilities within the cluster: Ahus Gardermoen, a satellite of
Akershus University Hospital, and Lovisenberg Rehabilitering AS, a rehabilitation clinic. No
university anchor is recorded here.
