Forlì is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with three Tier 1
co-location clusters, tied with Modena for the strongest anchor composition in Italy's
published set.

## Overview

Forlì is a comune and city in Emilia-Romagna, northern Italy, and is, together with Cesena,
the capital of the Province of Forlì-Cesena. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
