Pula is the largest city in Istria County, western Croatia, and the seventh-largest city in the
country. The market's underlying data records it under its official bilingual name, Pula - Pola.
It is anchored by Kaufland and Interspar (hypermarket), Bauhaus and Pevex (hardware), and
Decathlon and Hervis (sport) — one Regional-tier co-location cluster, the framework's highest
single-cluster tier. Trieste, Italy, sits 86.3 kilometres away by straight-line distance. The
market carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Pula recorded 52,220 residents at the 2021 census. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description
--> The city is renowned for its Roman architecture, most notably the Pula Arena, one of the
best-preserved Roman amphitheatres anywhere, alongside the Temple of Augustus and the Arch of the
Sergii. Pula served as the administrative centre of Istria from ancient Roman times until that
role passed to Pazin in 1991, reflecting centuries of continuous regional importance under Roman,
Venetian, Austro-Hungarian, and Italian rule.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 2.7 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and sport anchor. Kaufland
and Interspar, hypermarket chains comparable to Walmart or Kroger, sit alongside Bauhaus and Pevex
(hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and Decathlon and Hervis (sport).

Civic anchors include Pula General Hospital and two higher-education sites: the Pula Polytechnic
and a campus of Juraj Dobrila University of Pula, Istria's regional university.
