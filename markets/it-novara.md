Novara is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with one Tier 1 and two
Tier 3 co-location clusters.

## Overview

Novara is the capital city of the Province of Novara in the region of Piedmont in northern
Italy, west of Milan. With a population of 103,238, it is the second-largest city in Piedmont
after Turin. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
