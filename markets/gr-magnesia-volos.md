Volos is a port city on Greece's central-eastern coast. It is anchored by Sklavenitis
(hypermarket), Praktiker (hardware), and Kotsovolos (electronics) — one Regional-tier co-location
cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Thessaloniki sits 141.3 kilometres away by
straight-line distance. The market carries European Regional Market classification under the
co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Volos recorded 85,803 residents in its municipal unit and 139,670 across the wider municipality
at the 2021 census, in the Magnesia regional unit of Thessaly. <!-- wikipedia-sourced:
population/description --> The city serves as Thessaly's principal sea outlet, with a steel and
manufacturing base and the University of Thessaly anchoring its economy; much of its modern
building stock dates from reconstruction after severe 1955 earthquakes.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 4.0 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and electronics anchor.
Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds five locations
within the cluster; Praktiker (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and Kotsovolos
(electronics, comparable to Best Buy) each hold one.

Civic weight is substantial. The General Hospital of Volos "Achillopouleio" anchors medical care,
and the University of Thessaly maintains multiple faculties in the city, including economics,
architecture, civil and mechanical engineering, urban and regional planning, and agricultural
sciences.
