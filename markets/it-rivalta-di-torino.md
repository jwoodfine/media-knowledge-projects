Rivalta di Torino is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with one
Tier 1 and one Tier 3 co-location cluster, 13.7 kilometres from Turin — the shortest
metropolitan-reference distance of any Italian market in the published set.

## Overview

Rivalta di Torino is a comune in the Metropolitan City of Turin, in the Italian region of
Piedmont, located about 14 kilometres southwest of Turin in the valley of the Sangone.
