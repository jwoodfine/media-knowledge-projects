The **Bulgaria regional market index** lists the 11 Bulgarian markets that meet the co-location
system's composition gates. Bulgaria publishes its entire
qualifying pool: all 11 markets that cleared a composition gate are in the published set, with
none held back by the 400-market continental cap.

## Coverage

No rank or score is published for any market in this table. Every market below qualifies under
the framework's Regional Market composition gate — a hypermarket anchor paired with at least
two of {hardware, price club, lifestyle, electronics, sport} across the market's co-location
clusters. Full methodology:
[[about-regional-markets-system|Regional Markets Intelligence System]].

The metropolitan reference point recorded for each market is the nearest major metropolitan
centre in the framework's continental reference set — a straight-line (crow-flies) distance
measurement, not a drive-time or commuting estimate. It is not a claim that the market
functions as a commuter suburb of that centre. Ten of Bulgaria's 11 reference points sit within
the country — Varna for four entries, and Plovdiv and Sofia for three each. Only Ruse, on the
Danube, references a centre across a border, to Bucharest, Romania, 66 kilometres away.
Bulgaria's median reference distance is 79 kilometres and no market sits beyond 140 kilometres
from its reference point.

Catchment population has not been computed for any of the 11 Bulgarian markets in the current
dataset build — Bulgaria is one of six European countries without this figure in the 2026-08-07
release, alongside Croatia, Finland, Norway, Romania, and Sweden. This is a disclosed gap in
the underlying source data, stated plainly rather than presented as zero.

## Cluster composition

The 11 markets carry 11 co-location clusters between them — 11 Tier 1, and no Tier 2 or Tier 3
clusters at all. Bulgaria is the only country in either continental set where every market
qualifies on exactly one Tier 1 cluster and nothing else.
