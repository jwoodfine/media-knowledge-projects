The **Norway regional market index** lists the nine Norwegian markets that meet the co-location system's composition gates. The nine markets
run from Ullensaker, just north of Oslo, to Bodø, well north of the Arctic Circle — a spread far
wider than any other European country in the current dataset.

## Coverage

No rank or score is published for any market in this table. Every market below qualifies under
the framework's Regional Market composition gate — a hypermarket anchor paired with at least two
of {hardware, price club, lifestyle, electronics, sport} across the market's co-location clusters.
Full methodology: [[about-regional-markets-system|Regional Markets Intelligence System]].

The metropolitan reference point recorded for each market is the nearest major metropolitan
centre in the framework's continental reference set — a straight-line (crow-flies) distance
measurement, not a drive-time or commuting estimate. Eight of Norway's nine reference points sit
within Norway itself — Oslo (five entries) and Trondheim (three entries) — unlike Croatia and
Greece, where every reference point crossed an international border. Only Arendal's reference
point leaves the country, to Aalborg, Denmark, across the Skagerrak strait. Two markets — Bodø
(466.6 km) and Rana (365.6 km), both in Nordland county near or north of the Arctic Circle — carry
reference distances well beyond the rest of the set. This is a real consequence of how sparsely
major metropolitan centres are spaced along Norway's northern coastline, not a data gap.

Catchment population has not been computed for any of the nine Norwegian markets in the current
dataset build — Norway is one of six European countries without this figure in the 2026-08-07
release, alongside Bulgaria, Croatia, Finland, Romania, and Sweden. Each profile page states this
plainly rather than presenting an unavailable figure as zero or omitting the gap silently.

## Retail and Civic Anchors

OBS Coop anchors all nine clusters — the only chain present in every entry, a stronger single-chain
dominance than either Croatia's Kaufland/Pevex pairing or Greece's Sklavenitis. Elkjøp
(electronics) appears in eight of the nine, missing only from Ullensaker; OBS Bygg (hardware)
anchors seven of the nine, absent from Arendal and Bodø. Montér (hardware) appears in four
markets; XXL (sport) and Würth (industrial and MRO supply) each appear in three. Civic
infrastructure: a hospital anchors eight of the nine markets — every market except Steinkjer,
whose single cluster records no civic anchor at all in the current dataset, a real, disclosed gap
rather than an editorial omission. A university anchor appears in three markets — Drammen,
Elverum, and Ringsaker.

## AEC Data Coverage

Climate, seismic, and wildfire-hazard data has been computed for two of Norway's nine markets in
full — Arendal and Bodø — matching Greece's two-of-seven ratio. A third market, Ringsaker, carries
a partial record: its Local-tier cluster has a recorded environmental profile, but its
District-tier anchor cluster, roughly 16 kilometres away, does not, and the two are not close
enough to treat as the same site. The remaining six markets — Drammen, Elverum, Moss, Rana,
Steinkjer, and Ullensaker — have no AEC coverage at all in the current dataset build; see
[[gis-data-overview|GIS Data Overview]] for the AEC data pipeline.
