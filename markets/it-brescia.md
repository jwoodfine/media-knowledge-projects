Brescia is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with two Tier 1
co-location clusters, among the strongest anchor compositions in Italy's published set.

## Overview

Brescia is a city and comune in the region of Lombardy, Italy, situated at the foot of the
Alps a few kilometres from Lake Garda and Lake Iseo. With a population of 201,715, it is the
second-largest city in Lombardy behind Milan and the northernmost Italian city among those
with over 200,000 inhabitants. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
