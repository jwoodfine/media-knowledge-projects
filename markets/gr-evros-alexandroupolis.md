Alexandroupolis is a port city in northeastern Greece near the Turkish and Bulgarian borders. It
combines a District-tier cluster — Sklavenitis paired with Praktiker — and a Local-tier cluster —
Sklavenitis paired with Kotsovolos. Plovdiv, Bulgaria, sits 171.9 kilometres away by straight-line
distance, a cross-border reference reflecting the city's position in Thrace rather than a
commuting relationship. The market carries European Regional Market classification under the
co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Alexandroupolis recorded 71,751 residents at the 2021 census, and serves as capital of the Evros
regional unit in the Eastern Macedonia and Thrace region. <!-- wikipedia-sourced:
population/description --> The city's coastal position on the Thracian Sea has made it a growing
commercial and military port, with expanded strategic significance for Greece and NATO shipping
since 2022.

## Anchor Composition

The District-tier cluster spans 1.2 kilometres and pairs Sklavenitis (hypermarket, comparable to
Walmart or Kroger) with two Praktiker (hardware) locations. The Local-tier cluster, under two
kilometres away, pairs Sklavenitis with Kotsovolos (electronics, comparable to Best Buy).

Both clusters draw on the same civic anchors: the Old Hospital of Alexandroupolis and a nearby
military field hospital (216 ΚΙΧΝΕ), reflecting the city's dual commercial-military port role.

## AEC Data

Alexandroupolis sits in a Csa (hot-summer Mediterranean) Köppen climate zone, with an annual mean
temperature of 15.5°C. The site records 1,781 heating degree days and 869 cooling degree days a
year (base 18°C), average wind speed of 3.39 m/s, and annual solar irradiance (GHI) of
1,634.6 kWh/m².

Seismic and wildfire exposure are both real considerations for site planning here. Peak ground
acceleration reaches 0.1812g, and wildfire hazard is classified high. The surrounding ecoregion is
the Aegean and Western Turkey sclerophyllous and mixed forests, part of the Mediterranean Forests,
Woodlands & Scrub biome. These values are computed for the District-tier cluster; the Local-tier
cluster sits under two kilometres away, within the same climate and hazard zone.
