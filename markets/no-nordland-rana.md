Rana is a market in Norway's Helgeland region, just south of the Arctic Circle in Nordland
county. It is anchored by OBS Coop (hypermarket), two hardware chains — OBS Bygg and Montér — and
Elkjøp (electronics) across a single Regional-tier co-location cluster. Trondheim sits 365.6
kilometres away by straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set —
one of two Norwegian markets in the current dataset with a reference distance beyond 300
kilometres, both in Nordland county. The market carries European Regional Market classification
under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Rana recorded 25,994 residents in 2024, just south of the Arctic Circle in the Helgeland region.
<!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The municipality is Norway's fourth-largest by
area, at 4,460 square kilometres, and its administrative centre, Mo i Rana, houses a branch of the
National Library of Norway. Rana is also home to Svartisen, Norway's second-largest glacier, and
an extensive limestone cave system.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 1.5 kilometres and combines OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable
to Walmart or Kroger) with two hardware chains, OBS Bygg and Montér (comparable to Home Depot or
Lowe's), and Elkjøp (electronics, comparable to Best Buy).

Civic weight comes from Helgelandssykehuset Mo i Rana, the regional hospital serving the
Helgeland area. No university anchor is recorded here.
