Slavonski Brod is a river port city in eastern Croatia's Brod-Posavina County. It combines a
Regional-tier cluster — Kaufland paired with Pevex and Hervis — and a District-tier cluster —
Interspar paired with Pevex. Timișoara, Romania, sits 255.8 kilometres away by straight-line
distance, the longest metropolitan reference among Croatia's nine current entries. The market
carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Slavonski Brod recorded 49,891 residents at the 2021 census, on the Sava River. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The settlement traces back to Roman-era Marsonia
and was first documented under its medieval name in 1224. It developed as a fortified river town
during the Austro-Hungarian period, and its Vauban-style Brod Fortress was built to counter
Ottoman forces across the river. The city's economy today centres on agriculture and metal
manufacturing, including the Đuro Đaković industrial group.

## Anchor Composition

The Regional-tier cluster spans 1.7 kilometres and combines Kaufland (hypermarket, comparable to
Walmart or Kroger) with Pevex (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and Hervis (sport).
The District-tier cluster, under a kilometre away, pairs Interspar with Pevex.

Both clusters draw on the same civic anchors: the Brod Health Centre and a Faculty of Mechanical
Engineering materials-testing laboratory, a satellite facility rather than a full university
campus.
