Torrance, a mixed industrial-residential suburb in southwestern Los Angeles County, has a
single Regional-tier co-location cluster. It is not among the markets published for North
America under the confirmed 2026-08-07 dataset. The city combines a large regional mall with
a manufacturing and aerospace employment base uncommon among the covered markets with only
one cluster.

## Overview

Torrance recorded a population of 147,067 at the 2020 census (an estimated 139,576 by 2024)
across 20.516 square miles of land, approximately 20 miles (a general Wikipedia geographic
distance) southwest of downtown Los Angeles. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description
--> The city hosts one of the ten largest malls in the United States, the 2.5-million-square-
foot Del Amo Fashion Center, and oil refining remains economically important through a PBF
Energy facility. Torrance has the second-largest concentration of people of Japanese ancestry
of any U.S. city after Honolulu, alongside a significant Korean American population.

## Regional Market Standing

Torrance does not currently appear in the [[atlas-top-400-north-america|Top 400 Regional
Markets — North America]] list. This is not a data defect: the market is not among those
published under the confirmed dataset. Torrance has a single Regional-tier
co-location cluster.

Cluster-level detail — the specific retail and civic anchors composing Torrance's single
Regional-tier cluster — is pending the underlying GIS dataset and is not included in this
stub. See [[co-location-methodology|Co-location Methodology]] for how markets qualify for the
