Ringsaker is a market on the eastern shore of Lake Mjøsa, in Innlandet county. It combines a
District-tier cluster — OBS Coop paired with OBS Bygg — and a Local-tier cluster pairing OBS Bygg
with Elkjøp. Oslo sits 106.8 kilometres away by straight-line distance under the framework's
continental reference-point set. The market carries European Regional Market classification under
the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Ringsaker recorded 35,911 residents in 2025, on the eastern shore of Mjøsa, Norway's largest lake,
in the traditional Hedmarken district. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The
administrative centre is the town of Brumunddal; the municipality is primarily agricultural and
forested, rising into the Hedmarksvidda plateau in its upper reaches. Ringsaker carries deep
historical significance, appearing in medieval sagas concerning the Viking-era kings Harald
Hårfagre and Saint Olaf.

## Anchor Composition

The District-tier cluster spans 0.1 kilometres and pairs OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable to
Walmart or Kroger) with OBS Bygg (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's). Its civic anchors
are Sykehuset Innlandet Hamar, the regional hospital, and a University of Innlandet study centre,
both serving the nearby town of Hamar.

The Local-tier cluster sits roughly 16 kilometres away and pairs OBS Bygg with Elkjøp
(electronics, comparable to Best Buy). No civic anchor is recorded at this second cluster.

## AEC Data

Climate, seismic, and wildfire-hazard data has not been computed for Ringsaker's District-tier
anchor cluster in the current GIS dataset build. The Local-tier cluster, roughly 16 kilometres
away, does carry a recorded environmental profile.

It sits in a Dfc (subarctic) Köppen climate zone, with an annual mean temperature of 4.5°C. The
site records 4,963 heating degree days and 23 cooling degree days a year (base 18°C), average
wind speed of 1.52 m/s, and annual solar irradiance (GHI) of 903.1 kWh/m².

Peak ground acceleration reaches 0.0099g and wildfire hazard is classified low. The surrounding
ecoregion is the Scandinavian and Russian taiga, part of the Boreal Forests/Taiga biome. These
figures describe the Local-tier site specifically — at 16 kilometres, the distance is too great to
extend them to the District-tier anchor cluster with confidence.
