Playa del Carmen is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset — under the
Solidaridad municipality entry, whose seat is Playa del Carmen — with two Tier 1 and one
Tier 3 co-location cluster.

## Overview

Solidaridad is one of the eleven municipalities that make up the Mexican state of Quintana
Roo. Its municipal seat is the town of Playa del Carmen.
