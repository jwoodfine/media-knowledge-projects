Kalamata is the capital of Messenia and the second-largest city on the Peloponnese peninsula. It
is anchored by Sklavenitis (hypermarket), IKEA (lifestyle), and Kotsovolos (electronics) — one
Local-tier co-location cluster. Patras sits 138.4 kilometres away by straight-line distance — well
beyond typical suburban commuting range. Kalamata functions as its own regional centre rather than
a functional suburb of Patras, despite carrying Regional Market classification under the
framework's composition gate.

## Overview

Kalamata recorded 72,906 municipal residents at the 2021 census, on the Nedon River at the head
of the Messenian Gulf. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city functions as a
working Mediterranean port with a substantial agricultural export trade, and is internationally
known for the Kalamata olive and olive oil that carry its name.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 3.1 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, lifestyle, and electronics anchor.
Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds five locations
within the cluster, alongside one IKEA and one Kotsovolos electronics store.

Civic anchors include the University of Peloponnese, which maintains both its Antikalamos campus
and its School of Humanities and Cultural Studies in the city, and the General Hospital of
Messenia's Mental Health Centre.
