Šibenik is a historic coastal city in central Dalmatia, at the mouth of the Krka River on the
Adriatic Sea. It is anchored by Kaufland (hypermarket), Pevex (hardware), and Hervis (sport) — one
Regional-tier co-location cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Ancona, Italy,
sits 213.8 kilometres away by straight-line distance, crossing the Adriatic Sea rather than
reflecting a land commuting relationship. The market carries European Regional Market
classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Šibenik recorded 42,599 residents at the 2021 census. <!-- wikipedia-sourced:
population/description --> The city was first documented on Christmas Day 1066, in a land grant
from King Peter Krešimir IV, earning it the alternate name "Krešimir's Town." Its Cathedral of St.
James, built entirely of stone without mortar between 1431 and 1536, is a UNESCO World Heritage
Site. In 1895, Šibenik became the first city in the world with public street lighting powered by
alternating current, drawn from the nearby Jaruga hydroelectric plant.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 1.3 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and sport anchor. Kaufland,
a hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, sits alongside Pevex (hardware, comparable to
Home Depot or Lowe's) and Hervis (sport).

Civic anchors include the Šibenik-Knin County General Hospital and the Šibenik Open University, a
public adult and community-education institution rather than a full degree-granting university.
