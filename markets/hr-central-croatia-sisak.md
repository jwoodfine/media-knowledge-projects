Sisak is the administrative centre of Sisak-Moslavina County in central Croatia, at the confluence
of the Kupa, Sava, and Odra rivers. It is anchored by Kaufland and Interspar (hypermarket), Pevex
(hardware), and Hervis (sport) — one Regional-tier co-location cluster, the framework's highest
single-cluster tier. Graz, Austria, sits 189.2 kilometres away by straight-line distance. The
market carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Sisak recorded 40,121 residents at the 2021 census, roughly 57 kilometres southeast of Zagreb.
<!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city was originally a Roman settlement
known as Siscia, and gained lasting historical prominence for the 1593 Battle of Sisak, where
joint Croatian, Austrian, and Slovenian forces won an early victory against Ottoman expansion into
Europe. Sisak today is home to Croatia's largest oil refinery and functions as a significant river
port.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans just under two kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and sport
anchor. Kaufland and Interspar, hypermarket chains comparable to Walmart or Kroger, sit alongside
Pevex (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and Hervis (sport).

Civic anchors include the Dr. Ivo Pedišić General Hospital and the Faculty of Metallurgy, a
University of Zagreb faculty sited in Sisak.
