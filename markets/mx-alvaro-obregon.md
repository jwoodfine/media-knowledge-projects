Álvaro Obregón is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with three
Tier 1 co-location clusters — the strongest anchor composition of any market in Mexico's
published set.

## Overview

Álvaro Obregón is a borough (alcaldía) in Mexico City, containing a large portion of the
southwest part of the city. It had a 2020 census population of 759,137 and lies at an
elevation of 2,319 metres above sea level. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
