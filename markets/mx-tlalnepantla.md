Tlalnepantla de Baz is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with two
Tier 1 and one Tier 3 co-location cluster.

## Overview

Tlalnepantla de Baz is one of 125 municipalities of the State of Mexico, north of Mexico
City. The municipal seat and largest city in the municipality is the city of Tlalnepantla.
