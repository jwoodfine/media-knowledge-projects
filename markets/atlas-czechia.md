The **Czechia regional market index** lists the 27 Czech markets that meet the co-location
system's composition gates. It is the most tightly bounded
country entry in the European set: every Czech market sits within 101 kilometres of its
metropolitan reference point, and none exceeds that.

## Coverage

No rank or score is published for any market in this table. Every market below qualifies under
the framework's Regional Market composition gate — a hypermarket anchor paired with at least
two of {hardware, price club, lifestyle, electronics, sport} across the market's co-location
clusters. Full methodology:
[[about-regional-markets-system|Regional Markets Intelligence System]].

The metropolitan reference point recorded for each market is the nearest major metropolitan
centre in the framework's continental reference set — a straight-line (crow-flies) distance
measurement, not a drive-time or commuting estimate. It is not a claim that the market
functions as a commuter suburb of that centre. Nineteen of Czechia's 27 reference points sit
within the country — Brno for eight entries, Prague for seven, and Plzeň for four. The
remaining eight cross a border: five to Gliwice, Poland, two to Dresden, Germany, and one to
Linz, Austria. The Gliwice group is concentrated in the Moravian-Silesian region along the
Polish frontier. Czechia's median reference distance is 64 kilometres and 23 of its 27 markets
sit within 80 kilometres of their reference point.

Catchment population is recorded for 13 of the 27 Czech markets in the current dataset build,
and absent for the rest. Consumer-spend coverage matches it market for market. These are
disclosed gaps in the underlying source data; a market without a figure is stated as such
rather than presented as zero.

## Cluster composition

The 27 markets carry 36 co-location clusters between them — 21 Tier 1, one Tier 2, and 14 Tier
3. Twenty-one of the 27 carry at least one Tier 1 cluster, and five qualify on Tier 3 clusters
alone — the highest count of Tier 3-only markets of any country in the European set after
Italy.
