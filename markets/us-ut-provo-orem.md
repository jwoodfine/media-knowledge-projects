Provo, the larger of the twin Utah Valley cities of Provo and Orem, has one Regional-tier
co-location cluster. It is not among the markets published for North America under the
confirmed 2026-08-07 dataset. Brigham Young University anchors an unusually young,
fast-growing technology economy sometimes described as the "Silicon Slopes" cluster.

## Overview

Provo recorded a population of 115,162 at the 2020 census across 44.19 square miles, the
fourth-largest city in Utah, situated 43 miles (69 km, a general Wikipedia geographic
distance) south of Salt Lake City along the Wasatch Front between Orem to the north and
Springville to the south. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> Brigham Young
University is the dominant local employer, with roughly 35,000 students; the city also hosts
the LDS Church's largest Missionary Training Center. Provo functions as a technology hub —
Qualtrics and Nu Skin Enterprises are headquartered there alongside several billion-dollar
startups — with retail anchored by Provo Towne Centre and The Shops at Riverwoods. Orem
borders Provo directly to the north and is part of the same Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan
area, though Wikipedia's Provo article treats it as a distinct city rather than a single
combined entity.
