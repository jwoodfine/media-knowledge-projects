Varaždin is a city in northern Croatia known for its Baroque architecture and cultural heritage.
It is anchored by Kaufland (hypermarket), Bauhaus and Pevex (hardware), and Decathlon (sport) —
one Regional-tier co-location cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Graz, Austria,
sits 111.2 kilometres away by straight-line distance. The market carries European Regional Market
classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Varaždin recorded 43,782 residents at the 2021 census, 81 kilometres northeast of Zagreb. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city served as the capital of Croatia from 1756
to 1776, hosting the Croatian Sabor, before a devastating 1776 fire destroyed roughly 80 percent of
the city and prompted the government's relocation to Zagreb. Varaždin became the first Croatian
city designated a UNESCO Creative City, in the field of music, in 2023, reflecting a cultural
identity still built around its annual Baroque Evenings festival.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 5.7 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and sport anchor. Kaufland,
a hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds two locations within the cluster,
alongside Bauhaus and Pevex (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) and Decathlon (sport).

Civic anchors include Varaždin General Hospital and a dense higher-education presence: University
North, a standalone Croatian university, alongside the Faculty of Geotechnical Engineering and the
Faculty of Organization and Informatics, both University of Zagreb faculties sited in Varaždin.
