## Overview

Culiacán, officially Culiacán Rosales, is the capital and largest city of the state of
Sinaloa in northwestern Mexico. It is located within the Culiacán Municipality and was founded
in 1531 by Spanish conquistadors. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
