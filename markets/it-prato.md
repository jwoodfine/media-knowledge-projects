Prato is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with one Tier 1 and two
Tier 3 co-location clusters.

## Overview

Prato is a city and comune in the region of Tuscany, Italy, and is the capital of the Province
of Prato. The city lies in the northeast of Tuscany, roughly 15–17 kilometres from Florence.
With a population of 198,327 as of 2026, Prato is Tuscany's second-largest city after
Florence. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
