Elverum is a market in Norway's Østerdalen district, in Innlandet county. It is anchored by two
hardware chains, OBS Bygg and Montér, alongside OBS Coop (hypermarket) and Elkjøp (electronics)
across a single Regional-tier co-location cluster. Oslo sits 116.8 kilometres away by
straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set. The market carries
European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Elverum recorded 21,899 residents in 2025, at a crossroads in Innlandet county with the town of
Hamar to the west and Kongsvinger to the south. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
Elverum served as Norway's temporary capital during the German invasion of April 1940, when
Parliament issued the Elverum Authorization from the town on 9 April. The municipality maintains
sister-city relationships with towns in Denmark, Finland, Sweden, and Namibia.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 3.8 kilometres and combines OBS Bygg and Montér (hardware, each
comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) with OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable to Walmart or Kroger)
and Elkjøp (electronics, comparable to Best Buy).

Civic weight comes from Sykehuset Innlandet HF Elverum, the local hospital, alongside a University
of Innlandet study centre.
