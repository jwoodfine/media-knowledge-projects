Asti is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with one Tier 1 and one
Tier 2 co-location cluster — one of only two markets in Italy's published set carrying a
Tier 2 entry.

## Overview

Asti is a comune of 74,348 inhabitants (as of 2021) located in the Italian region of Piedmont,
about 55 kilometres east of Turin, in the plain of the Tanaro River. It is the capital of the
Province of Asti. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description -->
