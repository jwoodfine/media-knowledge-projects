Chania is the second-largest city on Crete and capital of the Chania regional unit. It is
anchored by Sklavenitis (hypermarket), IKEA (lifestyle), and Kotsovolos (electronics) — one
Local-tier co-location cluster. Heraklion sits 103.3 kilometres away by straight-line distance.
The market carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Chania recorded 111,375 municipal residents at the 2021 census, on Crete's northwest coast. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city's economy rests on olive and citrus
agriculture and on tourism, which expanded rapidly from the early 1970s onward, layered over a
townscape shaped by Minoan, Venetian, and Ottoman-era influences.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 2.8 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, lifestyle, and electronics anchor.
Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds three locations
within the cluster; IKEA and Kotsovolos (electronics, comparable to Best Buy) each hold one.

Civic anchors include Chania General Hospital "Agios Georgios" and the private Iassis clinic.
Higher education is well represented: the Technical University of Crete, the Hellenic
Mediterranean University's Chania department, and the Mediterranean Agronomic Institute of Chania
(CIHEAM) all sit within the catchment, alongside the Hellenic Naval Academy of Crete.
