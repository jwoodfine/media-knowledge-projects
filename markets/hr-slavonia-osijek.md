Osijek is the largest city in Croatia's Slavonia region and the fourth-largest city in the
country. It combines a Regional-tier cluster — Pevex paired with Kaufland and Interspar — and a
Local-tier cluster — Kaufland and Interspar paired with Decathlon and Hervis. Timișoara, Romania,
sits 199.0 kilometres away by straight-line distance, a cross-border reference reflecting Osijek's
position in eastern Croatia rather than a commuting relationship. The market carries European
Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Osijek recorded 96,313 residents at the 2021 census, on the southern bank of the Drava River. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city's origins trace to Roman times as Mursa, an
important military centre in the province of Pannonia; it was first recorded under its current
name in 1196. Osijek suffered extensive damage during the 1990s Croatian War of Independence but
has since been reconstructed, and now functions as a cultural and economic hub built around
Baroque landmarks including the 18th-century Tvrđa citadel.

## Anchor Composition

The Regional-tier cluster spans just over a kilometre and pairs Pevex (hardware, comparable to
Home Depot or Lowe's) with Kaufland and Interspar (hypermarket, comparable to Walmart or Kroger).
The Local-tier cluster, under three kilometres away, pairs Kaufland and Interspar with Decathlon
and Hervis (sport).

No civic anchor — hospital or university — is recorded for either of Osijek's clusters in the
current dataset. This is a real, disclosed gap in the underlying GIS data, not an editorial
omission.
