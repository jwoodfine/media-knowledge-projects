Gerakas is an eastern Athens suburb in the East Attica regional unit. It is anchored by
Sklavenitis (hypermarket), Praktiker (hardware), and MediaMarkt (electronics) — one Regional-tier
co-location cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Athens sits 12.3 kilometres
away by straight-line distance — the shortest metropolitan reference among Greece's seven current
entries. The market carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Gerakas recorded 33,856 residents at the 2021 census across roughly 6.6 square kilometres, and
has formed part of the Pallini municipality since a 2011 administrative merger. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The area shifted rapidly from farmland to dense
suburban housing after infrastructure built for the 2004 Athens Olympics, and is recorded in
antiquity as the birthplace of the philosopher Epicurus.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 2.6 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and electronics anchor —
the composition gate's qualifying threshold. Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to
Walmart or Kroger, holds multiple locations within the cluster. Praktiker (hardware, comparable to
Home Depot or Lowe's) and MediaMarkt (electronics, a pan-European counterpart to Best Buy)
complete the retail anchor set.

Civic infrastructure adds weight to the cluster. Sismanogleio General Hospital and Amalia Fleming
Hospital anchor the medical presence. Three more hospitals sit within the same Athens-area
catchment, including a military specialty hospital and a children's hospital.

## AEC Data

Gerakas sits in a Csa (hot-summer Mediterranean) Köppen climate zone, with an annual mean
temperature of 17.7°C. The site records 1,289 heating degree days and 1,185 cooling degree days a
year (base 18°C), average wind speed of 2.65 m/s, and annual solar irradiance (GHI) of
1,782.7 kWh/m².

Seismic and wildfire exposure both run high. Peak ground acceleration reaches 0.2012g — a real,
decision-relevant figure for insurance and construction-cost planning — and wildfire hazard is
classified very high. The surrounding ecoregion is the Aegean and Western Turkey sclerophyllous
and mixed forests, part of the Mediterranean Forests, Woodlands & Scrub biome.
