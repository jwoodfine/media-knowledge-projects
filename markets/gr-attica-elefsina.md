Elefsina is an industrial city on the Thriasio Plain west of Athens. It is anchored by
Sklavenitis (hypermarket), Praktiker (hardware), and Kotsovolos (electronics) — one Regional-tier
co-location cluster, the framework's highest single-cluster tier. Athens sits 17.9 kilometres
away by straight-line distance. The market carries European Regional Market classification under
the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Elefsina recorded 30,147 municipal residents at the 2021 census, in West Attica at the northern
end of the Saronic Gulf. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city hosts
Greece's largest oil refinery and is recorded in antiquity as the site of the Eleusinian Mysteries
and the birthplace of the playwright Aeschylus; it also carries the older English name Eleusis.

## Anchor Composition

The cluster spans 2.1 kilometres and combines a hypermarket, hardware, and electronics anchor.
Sklavenitis, a Greek hypermarket chain comparable to Walmart or Kroger, holds three locations
within the cluster. Praktiker's (hardware, comparable to Home Depot or Lowe's) nearest branch
sits in neighbouring Mandra, and Kotsovolos (electronics, comparable to Best Buy) completes the
set.

Civic infrastructure centres on healthcare. The Thriasio General Hospital of Elefsina and the
Elefsina Maternity Hospital both sit within the catchment.
