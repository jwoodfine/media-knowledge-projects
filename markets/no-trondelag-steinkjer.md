Steinkjer is a market on the inner Trondheimsfjord, in Trøndelag county. It is anchored by OBS
Coop (hypermarket), two hardware chains — OBS Bygg and Bygger'n — Elkjøp (electronics), and Würth
(industrial and MRO supply) across a single Regional-tier co-location cluster. Trondheim sits 84.1
kilometres away by straight-line distance under the framework's continental reference-point set.
The market carries European Regional Market classification under the co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Steinkjer recorded 24,032 residents in 2024, on the inner Trondheimsfjord in Trøndelag county.
<!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The town serves as the administrative centre
for Trøndelag county and holds rock carvings dating back 6,000 years. Steinkjer was extensively
rebuilt after bombing during the Second World War, and forestry and agriculture remain its primary
industries today.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 1.1 kilometres and combines OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable
to Walmart or Kroger) with two hardware chains, OBS Bygg and Bygger'n (comparable to Home Depot or
Lowe's). Elkjøp (electronics, comparable to Best Buy) and Würth (industrial and MRO supply,
comparable to Grainger) complete the roster.

No civic anchor — hospital or university — is recorded for Steinkjer's cluster in the current
dataset. This is a real, disclosed gap in the underlying GIS data, not an editorial omission.
