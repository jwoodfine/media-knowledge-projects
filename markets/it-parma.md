co-location clusters, among the strongest anchor compositions in Italy's published set.

## Overview

Parma is a city in the region of Emilia-Romagna in northern Italy, known for its architecture,
music, art, prosciutto, cheese, and surrounding countryside. With a population of 202,111 as
of 2025, Parma is the second most populous city in Emilia-Romagna after Bologna, the region's
capital, and is home to the University of Parma, one of the oldest universities in the world.
