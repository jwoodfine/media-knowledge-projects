Kenner, the largest incorporated suburb of New Orleans, has one District-tier and one
Local-tier co-location cluster. It is not among the markets published for North America
under the confirmed 2026-08-07 dataset. The city sits between Lake Pontchartrain and the
Mississippi River. Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport lies within its limits,
giving it an unusual mix of airport-driven commerce and suburban retail.

## Overview

Kenner recorded a population of 66,448 at the 2020 census across 15.11 square miles, making
it the sixth-most populous city in Louisiana. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description
--> The city sits in Jefferson Parish on the west side of Greater New Orleans. The
Pontchartrain Center, a convention facility opened in 1991, and retail destinations including
The Esplanade Mall and Treasure Chest Casino anchor local commerce. A section of north Kenner
known as "Little Honduras" reflects significant Hispanic business development.
