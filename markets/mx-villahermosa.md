Villahermosa is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset — under the
Centro municipality entry, whose seat is Villahermosa — with two Tier 1 and two Tier 3
co-location clusters.

## Overview

The Municipality of Centro is one of the 17 municipalities of the Mexican state of Tabasco.
Its municipal seat is the city of Villahermosa, the state capital.
