Tuxtla Gutiérrez is carried in the confirmed 2026-08-07 Regional Markets dataset with two
Tier 1 co-location clusters.

## Overview

Tuxtla Gutiérrez, or Tuxtla, is the capital and largest city of the Mexican state of Chiapas.
It is a government, commercial, and services-oriented city, and has had one of the
fastest-growing rates in Mexico over the last 40 years.
