Arendal is a city on Norway's southern coast in Agder county. It is anchored by OBS Coop
(hypermarket), XXL (sport), and Elkjøp (electronics) across a single Regional-tier co-location
cluster. Aalborg, Denmark, sits 173.0 kilometres away by straight-line distance under the
framework's continental reference-point set — the reference point crosses the Skagerrak strait
rather than a land border. The market carries European Regional Market classification under the
co-location system's composition gate.

## Overview

Arendal recorded 45,601 residents in 2025, on Norway's southern coast in Agder county. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city grew into a major shipping and
timber-export port through the nineteenth century; in 1880 it was the country's largest port by
tonnage handled. Arendal's preserved wooden merchant-era architecture anchors its historic core,
and the city now serves as the seat of the County Governor of Agder.

## Anchor Composition

The market's single cluster spans 0.3 kilometres and combines OBS Coop (hypermarket, comparable
to Walmart or Kroger) with XXL (sport) and Elkjøp (electronics, comparable to Best Buy).

Civic weight is limited to Sørlandet Sykehus Arendal, the regional hospital anchoring medical
infrastructure within the cluster; no university anchor is recorded here.

## AEC Data

Arendal's cluster sits in a Dfb (warm-summer humid continental) Köppen climate zone, with an
annual mean temperature of 8.0°C. The site records 3,692 heating degree days and 35 cooling degree
days a year (base 18°C), average wind speed of 3.74 m/s, and annual solar irradiance (GHI) of
1,012.5 kWh/m².

Seismic exposure is low, with peak ground acceleration reaching 0.009g, and wildfire hazard is
classified low. The surrounding ecoregion is the Sarmatic mixed forests, part of the Temperate
Broadleaf & Mixed Forests biome.
